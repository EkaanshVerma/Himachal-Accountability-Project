About The Project

Making Himachal's democracy work for every citizen, constituency, and ward

Our Work

Himachal Pradesh spans 12 districts, 68 assembly constituencies, and over 7.5 million citizens. Most families live in the mountain towns and rural panchayats of their birth. Their future—and the state's prosperity—is decided district by district, road by road, and pipeline by pipeline.

Yet for decades, Himachal has lacked a transparent, continuous, and verifiable record of how well each district and elected representative delivers essential public services. RTI replies sit in private inboxes. Social media posts get buried. News coverage moves on. The Himachal Accountability Project (HAP) fills that gap.

We systematically monitor public delivery across all 12 districts, drawing from community field verifications, official assembly records, and municipal reports. Every district is evaluated across five fundamental public accountability pillars: Roads & Transit, Drinking Water, Healthcare Access, Power Supply, and Youth Opportunity.

The composite scores serve as a non-partisan diagnostic: they celebrate district administrative achievements, pinpoint systemic failure points, and track remediation progress over time.

Currently, 69% of verified citizen grievances in Himachal Pradesh remain unaddressed by local administrations. From collapsed retaining walls on NH-154A to dry irrigation channels in Kangra, HAP documents the exact duration and administrative pendency of every issue.

For all 68 MLAs and 4 Lok Sabha MPs, we track assembly constituency neglect rates, unaddressed reports, and provide one-click civic outreach tools allowing citizens to petition their elected representatives directly via WhatsApp or Email.

The intent is to bridge verified ground data with democratic action so that public resources reach the communities that need them most, and every elected representative is held accountable for the fundamental quality of life in their constituency.

Official Position · The Line We Hold

Our goal is to make ground realities transparent so that public administration works faster and better for the people of Himachal.”

Operating Principles

Four commitments we never compromise on

PRINCIPLE — 01

Zero Political Funding

We decline contributions from political parties, candidates, elected officials, and their affiliates—BJP, Congress, AAP, or otherwise. Independence is structural: the data only matters if no one can buy what it says.

PRINCIPLE — 02

Equal Scrutiny Across Parties

HAP documents administrative neglect regardless of who is in power. If a road has been damaged across multiple tenures, all records remain on the ledger. Selective scrutiny is just opposition research with a civic veneer.

PRINCIPLE — 03

Data Precedes Claims

Every report, score, and civic petition is anchored in verified ground evidence: GPS location, constituency, pin code, duration, and photographic proof. We do not publish unverified allegations.

PRINCIPLE — 04

Open Public Ledger

Our records and methodology are fully open to the public. There is no private tier that contradicts the public ledger. Transparency is the only currency that builds long-term institutional trust.

Independent Civic Funding

This work exists because citizens and independent supporters fund it.

The Himachal Accountability Project began as an entirely self-funded citizen initiative. The foundational proof of concept, live mapping software, data pipelines, and field verification networks were built through the personal resources of its founder.

We decline all contributions from political parties, election candidates, and sitting government representatives—BJP, Congress, AAP, or otherwise. That independence is foundational: our ledger only has authority if no political entity can alter what it displays.

If you believe Himachal Pradesh deserves transparent, non-partisan, and uncompromised civic accountability, we invite you to support our operations. Your contribution directly funds ongoing cloud infrastructure, ground verification travel to remote panchayats, and RTI filing fees.

Donation Principles & Governance Terms
  • We strictly accept contributions from Indian citizens and non-partisan civil society partners.
  • We accept zero funding from political parties, partisan political trusts, or sitting candidates.
  • Contributions do not entitle any donor to editorial control, report suppression, or preferential score adjustments.
  • We publish regular transparency summaries detailing operational expenditures, server costs, and field verification allocations.
  • All supporter information is held securely and never sold, leased, or shared with commercial entities.
